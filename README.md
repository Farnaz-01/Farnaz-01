## 🧭 &nbsp;About Me

🎓 **Computer Science Student** at American International University – Bangladesh (AIUB), Dhaka 🇧🇩  

🔬 **I don’t just read research papers** — I implement them from scratch.  
From understanding the mathematics to turning them into clean, working code in PyTorch.

🧠 My current focus areas include:  
• Transformers & Attention Mechanisms  
• Diffusion Models  
• Reinforcement Learning  
• Efficient AI for resource-constrained environments  

🌱 **Right now** I’m deep into building fully reproducible ML experiments and documenting the real challenges that papers usually don’t mention.

💡 **Core Belief**  
*The best way to truly learn something is to implement it.*  

🤝 **Always open to** research collaborations, paper study groups, thoughtful discussions, and helping fellow students.
